  1. What this is

    We don’t lock practices into long contracts. This page is our agreement with you, written in plain English on purpose. There is no separate contract and no fine print anywhere else. If you want a signed copy for your records, request one at the bottom of this page.

  2. Who it covers

    This guarantee applies to practices on our top plan, the one where we also work on your website. It doesn’t apply to free listings or to our other plans.

  3. Our promise

    If AI isn’t naming your practice 90 days after the clock starts, we stop billing you and keep working until it does. Billing resumes the month after you’re named, and you’ll see the proof first.

  4. When the clock starts

    The 90 days start on the later of two dates: the day onboarding is complete, meaning we have access to your Google profile and website and you’ve confirmed your facts, or the last day you edited your registry record. Changing your record restarts the clock, because AI has to learn the new facts.

  5. What counts as being named

    Your practice is named in an answer from ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity or Google AI Overviews when asked where to get one of the treatments or services listed on your registry record, in your city. We test in a fresh session from your area and share the screenshot in your monthly Proof Report.

  6. What we do every month

    We keep your facts in sync across Google, Apple Maps, Yelp and the directories AI reads. We answer new reviews, publish posts and articles about your treatments, and keep your website readable by AI. Nothing goes live without your approval.

  7. What we need from you

    You review our drafts within 3 business days. You keep our access to your Google profile and website. Your account stays current, and your Google profile stays in good standing.

  8. Your data

    We sign a HIPAA business associate agreement with you, encrypt your data with AES-256, and carry $750K in cyber insurance in case anything ever goes wrong. Our team is based in the US.

  9. Billing and cancelling

    Billing is month to month. You can cancel any time, with no cancellation fee. You keep everything we made for you, and it stays in your name.

  10. Limits

    AI tools are owned and run by other companies, and their answers can vary by person, place and time. We can’t control them, so this guarantee is limited to what’s written here: we stop billing and keep working. It doesn’t cover refunds of past payments.

  11. Changes to these terms

    If we ever change these terms, we’ll tell you at least 30 days before. The version in your signed copy applies until you agree to a new one.
