What patients ask AI before they book a consult

Their eyesight is on the line, so patients ask AI question after question. Every answer is built from what AI can verify about you.

  • “Who is the best LASIK surgeon near me?”

    AI needs: Your procedures named clearly on your site and profiles, with reviews that mention them.

  • “Is this eye surgeon board certified and fellowship trained?”

    AI needs: Your board certification and fellowship stated the same way on every source AI checks.

  • “How much does LASIK cost here?”

    AI needs: Price ranges or financing details AI can quote instead of guessing.

  • “Should I pay for a premium lens with my cataract surgery?”

    AI needs: Plain-English pages on your lens options, written and signed by your surgeons.

  • “Am I a candidate for LASIK, PRK, SMILE or ICL?”

    AI needs: Pages that show depth in each procedure, not just a list of services.

  • “What is recovery like after cataract surgery?”

    AI needs: Plain-English answers on your own site, with your name on them.

We build and maintain those answers for you, so AI has a reason to say your name.

Every eye procedure gets a different AI question

A cataract patient and a LASIK patient ask AI very different things. AI can only answer with what it can verify about you, procedure by procedure.

  • Cataract surgery

    “Who is the best cataract surgeon near me, and do they take Medicare?”

    AI has to find: Each surgeon's cataract focus stated plainly, insurance and Medicare details, and every office listed the same way.

  • Premium lenses

    “Is a multifocal or light adjustable lens worth the extra cost?”

    AI has to find: Lens options explained in plain English, with what insurance covers and what is out of pocket.

  • LASIK

    “Who does bladeless LASIK near me, and what does it cost?”

    AI has to find: The laser platforms you actually use, price ranges or financing, and how candidacy is decided.

  • SMILE, PRK and ICL

    “I was told I'm not a LASIK candidate. What else is there?”

    AI has to find: A page for each alternative, so AI can match the question to your surgeons.

  • Refractive lens exchange

    “Should I do lens replacement instead of LASIK at 50?”

    AI has to find: Age-based guidance AI can quote, with your surgeon's name on it.

  • Cornea and keratoconus

    “Who does corneal cross-linking or DMEK near me?”

    AI has to find: Fellowship training and subspecialty listed the same way on every source.

  • Glaucoma and MIGS

    “Can my glaucoma be treated during cataract surgery?”

    AI has to find: Combined-procedure pages and the surgeons who perform them.

  • Dry eye

    “Who treats severe dry eye beyond eye drops?”

    AI has to find: The treatments and devices you offer, office by office.

Questions eye surgeons ask us

How does ChatGPT decide which cataract or LASIK surgeon to recommend?

It names surgeons it can verify: the same practice facts on Google, directories and review sites, a board certification and fellowship it can confirm, procedures it can match to the question, and recent reviews. When those sources disagree or say little, it names someone else.

We have several surgeons and offices. How do you handle that?

Every surgeon and every office gets its own record with matching names, credentials and hours. That stops AI from merging your doctors, sending patients to the wrong office, or confusing you with a similarly named practice.

Can you cover premium lens questions without promising outcomes?

Yes. We write plain-English pages on lens options and costs that your surgeons review and approve. We never promise results, and we never rank one manufacturer's lens against another.

Do you work with our referring optometrists?

We make your co-management details clear and consistent, so a referred patient can confirm you quickly. We do not contact your referring doctors unless you ask us to.

How do you keep review replies HIPAA-safe?

Our replies never confirm that someone is a patient and never mention a procedure or any health details. Every reply waits for your approval before it posts.

Will this work with our current marketing agency?

Yes. We do not replace your website, ads or social media. We handle the listings, reviews, credentials and monthly AI checks that agencies usually leave alone, and we share our reports with your agency.

Which AI tools do you track?

ChatGPT, Google Gemini and AI Overviews, Claude and Perplexity. Your monthly report shows what each one says about your surgeons and what changed.

How long until AI starts naming our practice?

Our goal is about 3 months, and some practices see changes sooner. If AI is not naming you in 90 days, we keep working for free until it does.
